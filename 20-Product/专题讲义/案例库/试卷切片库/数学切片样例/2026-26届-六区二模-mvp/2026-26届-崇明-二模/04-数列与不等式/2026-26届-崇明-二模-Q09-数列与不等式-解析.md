由题意, ${a}_{1}$ 为 ${x}^{3}$ 的系数, ${\left( x - 1\right) }^{3}$ 和 ${\left( x + 1\right) }^{4}$ 的展开式中都包含 ${x}^{3}$ 项,

故 ${a}_{1}{x}^{3} = {\mathrm{C}}_{3}^{0}{x}^{3} + {\mathrm{C}}_{4}^{1}{x}^{3} \times  1 = 5{x}^{3}$ ,

故 ${a}_{1} = 5$
