$y = \cos \left( {\frac{\pi }{2} - {2x}}\right)  = \sin {2x}$ , $T = \frac{2\pi }{2} = \pi$ ,故最小正周期为 $\pi$ ,

设 $y = f\left( x\right)  = \sin {2x}, f\left( {-x}\right)  = \sin \left( {-{2x}}\right)  =  - \sin {2x} =  - f\left( x\right)$ ,

故 $y = f\left( x\right)  = \sin {2x}$ 为奇函数,故选项 $\mathrm{A}$ 正确.
