$\overrightarrow{a}$ 在 $\overrightarrow{b}$ 方向上的数量投影为 $\frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot  \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b}\right| } = \frac{3\cos x + \sqrt{3}\sin x}{2\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}\cos \left( {x - \frac{\pi }{6}}\right) }{2\sqrt{3}} = \cos \left( {x - \frac{\pi }{6}}\right)$ , $x \in  \left\lbrack  {0,\frac{\pi }{2}}\right\rbrack  ,\;x - \frac{\pi }{6} \in  \left\lbrack  {-\frac{\pi }{6},\frac{\pi }{3}}\right\rbrack  ,\;\cos \left( {x - \frac{\pi }{6}}\right)  \in  \left\lbrack  {\frac{1}{2},1}\right\rbrack  .$
