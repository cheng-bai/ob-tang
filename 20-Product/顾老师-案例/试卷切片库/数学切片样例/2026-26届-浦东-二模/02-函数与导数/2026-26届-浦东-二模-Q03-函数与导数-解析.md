当 $a < 0$ 时， $f\left( a\right)  = {a}^{\frac{1}{3}} =  - 2, a = {\left( -2\right) }^{3} =  - 8$
当 $a \geq  0$ 时, $f\left( a\right)  = a + 1 =  - 2, a =  - 3$ (舍)
$\therefore a =  - 8$
