设等差数列 $\left\{  {a}_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ,依题意, $d = \frac{{a}_{26} - {a}_{6}}{{26} - 6} = \frac{{11} - 1}{20} = \frac{1}{2}$ ,

则 ${a}_{2026} = {a}_{6} + \left( {{2026} - 6}\right)  \times  d = 1 + {2020} \times  \frac{1}{2} = {1011}$ .
