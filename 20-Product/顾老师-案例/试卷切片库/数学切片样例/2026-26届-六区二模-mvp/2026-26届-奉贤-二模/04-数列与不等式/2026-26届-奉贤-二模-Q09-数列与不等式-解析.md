依题意可得 $P\left( A\right)  = \frac{{\mathrm{C}}_{10}^{3} - {\mathrm{C}}_{6}^{3}}{{\mathrm{C}}_{10}^{3}} = \frac{5}{6},\;P\left( {AB}\right)  = \frac{{\mathrm{C}}_{4}^{2}{\mathrm{C}}_{6}^{1}}{{\mathrm{C}}_{10}^{3}} = \frac{3}{10}$ ,

所以 $P\left( {B \mid  A}\right)  = \frac{P\left( {AB}\right) }{P\left( A\right) } = \frac{\frac{3}{10}}{\frac{5}{6}} = \frac{9}{25}$ .
