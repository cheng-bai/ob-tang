因为事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 独立,事件 $A$ 发生的概率 $P\left( A\right)  = {0.2}$ ,事件 $B$ 发生的概率 $P\left( B\right)  = {0.5}$ ,

$\therefore P\left( {A \cap  \bar{B}}\right)  = P\left( A\right) P\left( \bar{B}\right)  = {0.2} \times  \left( {1 - {0.5}}\right)  = {0.2} \times  {0.5} = {0.1}$ .
