由 $y = \frac{3}{x + 1} + \ln x$ ,所以 ${y}^{\prime } =  - \frac{3}{{\left( x + 1\right) }^{2}} + \frac{1}{x}$ ,

所以曲线在 $x = 2$ 处的切线斜率为: $k = {\left. {y}^{\prime }\right| }_{x = 2} =  - \frac{3}{{\left( 2 + 1\right) }^{2}} + \frac{1}{2} =  - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6}$ .
